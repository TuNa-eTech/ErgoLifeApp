### Description (Max 4000 chars)
ErgoLife is your ultimate companion for building better habits and a healthier lifestyle through the power of gamification.

**Transform Your Habits into an Adventure**
Turn your daily tasks into an exciting journey across the sea. Each habit you complete propels you forward, allowing you to visit unique islands, unlock hidden treasures, and visualize your progress on a beautiful, interactive map.

**Key Features:**

*   **Gamified Journey**: Experience personal growth as a voyage. Your consistency is rewarded with progress on your map, unlocking new islands and achievements.
*   **House & Community**: You don't have to travel alone. Create or join a "House" to share your journey with friends and family. Collaborate, compete, and motivate each other to achieve your goals.
*   **Visual Statistics**: Stay on top of your performance with intuitive charts and insights. Track your "Top Tasks," view your streak, and see your daily improvements at a glance.
*   **Ergo Coach**: Receive personalized guidance and motivation from your Ergo Coach to keep you on the right path.
*   **Privacy First**: Your data is yours. We prioritize your privacy and ensure your personal journey remains secure.

Start your ErgoLife today and make self-improvement the most exciting part of your day!
